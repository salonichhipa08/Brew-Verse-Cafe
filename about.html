About Brew Verse Cafe

Brew Verse was founded in 2026 with a quiet but determined belief: India deserves great coffee, served with honesty, intention, and a feeling of home. What began as a small Brew Verse has grown into a café culture that celebrates the simple pleasures of everyday life. We source 80+ scored coffees from India’s most dedicated farms, roast them fresh in-house, and serve them in cafés built around modern Indian warmth, spaces designed for conversations, comfort, and community. Our journey has always been guided by one promise: to honour the entire journey from farm to cup. We work closely with farmers, pay fair and premium prices for quality, roast each batch with respect for the bean, and serve coffee with the calmness and care that define Brew Verse. Today, Brew Verse is more than a café. It is a feeling of familiarity, of warmth, of being remembered.

About the Founder

At 18, growing up in Jaipur, I always dreamed of creating a place where people could slow down and enjoy life's little moments. One evening, while watching the sunset with a cup of coffee, I thought about how every coffee has its own story, just like every person. That idea made me imagine coffee as a universe waiting to be explored.

After days of brainstorming, I combined the words "Brew", representing the art of coffee, and "Verse", symbolizing a universe of stories, creativity, and connections. The moment I said Brew Verse out loud, I knew I had found the perfect name.

Today, Brew Verse represents more than just a café. It's a cozy corner where every cup begins a new conversation, every visit creates a memory, and every customer becomes a part of our story.
